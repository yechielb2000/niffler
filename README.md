# Niffler

Niffler is a distributed operations platform designed to provide visibility, coordination, and control across remote systems.

Inspired by the magical creature known for finding hidden treasures, Niffler focuses on discovering valuable information, bringing it back to a central location, and presenting it in a way that allows operators to understand what is happening across their environment.

At its core, Niffler consists of two worlds:

- A lightweight presence that lives close to the action.
- A central command platform where information is collected, organized, and acted upon.

The goal is not simply to execute tasks remotely, but to build a complete picture of a distributed environment over time. Every interaction contributes to a growing understanding of the systems being managed.
